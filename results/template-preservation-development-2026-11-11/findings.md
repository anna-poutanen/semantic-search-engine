## What actually changed

The rule inspects complete canonical Python definitions and protects baseline
rows directly returning an f-string with an immediate formatted call. It makes
no runtime dispatch, mutation, code-generation or query-relevance assertion.
Its only choice is between the complete exact saved prior-role proposal and the
complete exact coverage response; every field, source byte, score, provenance,
timing envelope and JSON mapping order of the chosen operand stays unchanged.
The [prospectively frozen protocol](../../docs/template-preservation-protocol.md)
defines the AST scope, exclusions, partial-window behavior and witnesses.

Of 477 decisions, 471 accept prior-role and six fall back to coverage because a
protected row changed. All six are the same attrs query across dense/hybrid and
three repetitions: **two scored occurrences and four integrity repetitions**.
Only hybrid becomes newly complete.

is not resident search or native warm latency and establishes no speedup.
