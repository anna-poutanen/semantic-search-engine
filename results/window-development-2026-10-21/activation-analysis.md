# Complementary-window activation and stage analysis

Post hoc descriptive analysis of the fixed audited development outcomes. This derived report is separate from the original independent audit; it binds the exact raw, plan, summary and audit bytes in activation-analysis.json. No new variant, model, retrieval or network call was made.

The 365 observed query/order case rows share 187 distinct dataset/query units; five conditions produce 1825 response records. Reused studies and retrieval conditions are not independent queries.

| Condition | Selected composite excerpts / active case rows | Final composite excerpts / active case rows | Distinct query units with final composites |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| connected_union | 79 / 72 | 28 / 28 | 11 |
| guarded_continuation | 0 / 0 | 4 / 4 | 2 |
| guarded_connected_union | 79 / 72 | 7 / 7 | 5 |

Connected unions reach the initial eligible/packed stream in 22 case rows. Covered-row backfill brings final composites to 28 excerpts in 28 case rows, one each. The source guard evaluates 28 nontrivial proposal case rows, rejects 21 and accepts 7 (one composite each, five distinct query units). Its 344 overall accepts also include 337 trivial responses without a proposed delivered composite. All seven accepted composites add **zero actual physical source lines** over coverage_control: their delivered prefixes stop before the companion contributes novel source.

Forward continuation makes 77 trials: 50 have no character suffix, 23 have no token suffix, and 4 apply. The four applications add six line occurrences across two reused Zod query units: tree-error paths (two lines each in language/native dense studies) and a scoped absent remote-reference case (one line each). Every application extends a clipped row through its own original full window; none adds a different companion window. No new evidence group or complete query results.

The coverage control delivers 377 of 456 required group occurrences and completes 242 of 298 positive case rows. Its 79 missing group occurrences split as follows:

| First absent recorded stage | Group occurrences |
|---|---:|
| selected_not_eligible | 61 |
| absent_from_replay_input_pool | 16 |
| representative_window_dedup | 1 |
| packing_budget | 1 |

The 61 selected-but-not-eligible occurrences mean a full definition representative covers the evidence, but no covering representative enters the eight-row eligible stream. Of these, 53 are at selected ranks greater than 8 and 8 are in positions 6–8 displaced by the original dependency allocation. This category excludes the separate representative-window and packing misses. The 16 input-pool misses comprise 12 in original K50 orders and 4 in scored K24 orders; K24 absence is not proof that initial retrieval never found the code.

The sole representative-window miss is the urllib3 file-body rewind query under hybrid retrieval. Connected selection recovers its full evidence from raw rank 22 into selected rank 11, but it still does not enter the returned budget. Joining does not address that later selection bottleneck.

The sole regression is Zod local JSON-schema references under BM25. The irrelevant first finalize excerpt grows from 2968 to 7444 characters. At 11994 source characters, packing holds only that union and a 4550-character prefix of extractDefs; resolveRef, present at raw rank 6 and eligible 5, is never packed. This is the character cap: the proposal uses 3653 tokens and does not hit token clipping. The guarded condition rejects it and returns the coverage reference, where resolveRef is complete at returned rank 3.

All three candidates recover zero new groups and zero new complete queries over coverage_control. Their two gains over legacy were already achieved by that control. The unguarded union loses one group, complete case and single-file hit; both guarded conditions avoid that regression.

This closes the fixed joining hypothesis with a negative finding. Defaults should stay unchanged and the fresh repository should remain unqueried. A future, separately preregistered study should prioritize complementary **definitions/query aspects** within the eight-row budget: most remaining evidence is either selected too low or absent from the replay input pool, rather than hidden behind the representative window of a long definition.

All activation counts, strata, missing-group records, accepted composites, continuation applications and regression stage bounds are retained in activation-analysis.json.
